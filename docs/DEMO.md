# Submission draft
**Title:** FieldShift — Grow with tomorrow in mind

**Problem:** Water pressure, repeated crops and uncertain seasons make rotation choices difficult. Satellite-scale data alone cannot tell a farmer what to plant.

**Solution:** A transparent planning workspace that combines NASA POWER observations with local soil, irrigation and crop choices. Farmers compare their baseline with three alternatives over two years, explore dry/warm scenarios, inspect trade-offs, and export the evidence behind the plan.

**NASA data:** POWER T2M, PRECTOTCORR and GWETROOT, sourced from MERRA-2 reanalysis. Exact query, data units, resolution caveats and the unmodified response are included.

**Distinctive value:** A baseline-first comparison, honest separation of observations and assumptions, a reproducible report, and a useful demo that still works when upstream retrieval is unavailable.

**Status:** Functional educational prototype with regional Punjab/Haryana crop choices. No validated yield, profit or field-level risk predictions. Vegetation input is manual, and direct SMAP/GPM/Landsat integrations remain future work.
